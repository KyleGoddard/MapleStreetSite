Halloween night

It's Halloween night on Maple Street!

Grab your friends and roam the town. There's plenty to see, and even more to hear.

Fill your bags with candy, but don't dawdle: porch lights go out as the night wears on.

Keep an eye out for folks who need a hand. There are mysteries to solve out there.

And mind yourself if you stay out late… the later it gets, the spookier it gets.

Fill your bags

Knock on every lit door in town. Ninety-three places are handing out candy: houses, car trunks, candy tables, the Quik Mart, the fire station, the farm stand, the block party and the middle of the corn maze.

Every yard is decorated differently, and the whole neighbourhood is out for the night.

Lend a hand

Anyone with a gold ! overhead needs help. Find the little boy lost in the corn maze. Bring Biscuit the dog home. Track down a ghost's missing skull.

Every good deed earns an artifact for your collection.

Trick, then treat

Some houses want a trick first. Spin the wheel, bob for apples, play the shell game or try the trick-or-treat slots.

Win and everyone gets extra candy. Bite the apple with the worm in it, and, well.

The night wears on

Five in the evening to the stroke of midnight, in about half an hour.

  1. 5:00 PM

    Head out

    Porch lights are on and the streets are full of trick-or-treaters.

  2. 9:00 PM

    The bell tolls

    The clock tower strikes nine. House by house, the lights start going out.

  3. 10:45 PM

    Teenagers

    A pack of them is after your candy. Run for a porch that still has its light on.

  4. Midnight

    Bags down

    Count your haul, earn your Halloween title and chase a spot on the high score board.
