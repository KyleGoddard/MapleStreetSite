Privacy policy

Effective 21 October 2026

The short version

Maple Street doesn't collect any personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The game never connects to the internet, and everything it remembers stays on your device.

Who I am

Maple Street is made by Kyle Goddard, an independent developer. In this policy, "I" and "me" mean Kyle. This policy covers the Maple Street game on every platform it's released on, and this website, maplestreetgame.com.

What the game keeps on your device

The game saves a few small files so it can remember things between nights:

  • The high score board: the initials you enter, your score and your Halloween title.
  • The achievements you've earned.
  • The costumes you've unlocked, and who's wearing what.

These files stay on your device. The game has no way to send them anywhere, and I never see them.

What the game doesn't do

  • It doesn't ask for your name, email address, location, contacts or photos, or for access to your camera, microphone or anything else on your device.
  • It doesn't contain advertising, analytics or crash-reporting tools, or any other third-party code that collects data.
  • It doesn't use advertising identifiers, and it doesn't track you across other apps or websites.
  • There's nothing to buy inside the game.

The app stores

You get Maple Street from the App Store, Google Play or Steam. They handle downloads and payments, and they collect information of their own under their own privacy policies. They give me only summary figures, such as how many people downloaded the game, and crash reports from players who have chosen to share them with developers. None of this tells me who you are.

If your device backs up app data, for example to iCloud, your save files may be included in that backup. Backups are handled by Apple or Google under your own account settings.

Deleting your saves

On a phone or tablet, deleting the game deletes its save files. On a computer, uninstalling the game can leave them behind. To remove them, delete this folder:

  • Windows: %APPDATA%\Godot\app_userdata\Maple Street
  • Mac: ~/Library/Application Support/Godot/app_userdata/Maple Street
  • Linux: ~/.local/share/godot/app_userdata/Maple Street

This website

maplestreetgame.com doesn't use cookies, analytics or trackers, and it doesn't load anything from other websites. It's hosted by GitHub Pages, which may log visitors' IP addresses to keep its service secure, as described in GitHub's privacy statement. I don't have access to those logs.

If you write to me

The contact form below asks for your email address and your message, and your name if you'd like to give it. It's delivered to my inbox by Web3Forms, a form delivery service, under its own privacy policy.

I use what you send only to reply to you. I won't add you to a mailing list or pass your message on to anyone else. I delete messages once we're done talking, or sooner if you ask.

Children

Maple Street is a family-friendly game for everyone. Because the game collects nothing, children can play it without sharing any personal information.

If you're under 13, please ask a parent or guardian before using the contact form. If I find out that a child under 13 has sent me personal information without a parent's permission, I'll delete it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, laws such as the GDPR or the California Consumer Privacy Act give you rights over your personal information, including the right to see it, correct it or have it deleted. The game gives me nothing to see, correct or delete. For anything you've sent me through the contact form, just ask and I'll do it.

I don't sell or share anyone's personal information.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. If a future version of the game ever needs to collect any information, I'll update this policy, and the privacy details on each store page, before that version is released.

Contact me

Questions about privacy, or anything else about Maple Street? Send me a message.
